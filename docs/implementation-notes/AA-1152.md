# AA-1152 — optional activities per day, the agency's own prices (TripPlanner part)

Slice L9 of the root `docs/audits/2026-11-19-S285-session/advisory-r2-plan.md` (Jira PR-29 T6 and B2 option C). The
CRM drawer and price computation, Booking's `GET /add-ons`, `bk-070` and the root contracts ship in their own PRs.

## Decisions

- **Day places**: the sleep place first = the `overnight_destination_id` of the day's last stop on an AA tour day
  (`catalog.v_tour_day_route`, granted to `tripplanner`), else the day's last stop; then the stops in order. Each
  add-on once a day, at most 12 (the lead's `DayPlan.addons` bound).
- **Picks are trip events** (`add_addon` / `remove_addon`, tp-037 widens the CHECK): they survive a reload, reach the
  lead, and are erased with the tenant's `trip_events`. A pick is checked against the day's offered list and takes
  AA's name from the view, never from the browser.
- **The lead**: `day_plan[].addons` (sv: id + AA's name, never a price); a lead with an add-on says version "1.2",
  every other one keeps "1.0", so a consumer validating against an older copy keeps taking leads without add-ons.
- **Agency prices are pushed by the CRM** (tp-036 `tripplanner.agency_prices`, one row per tenant, scope spelling of
  tp-035, newest `computed_at` wins): no AA app reads `crm` (root ADR 0008a), and TripPlanner never sees AA's B2B price
  or the markup. Read fail soft (42P01 / 42501 = no price). AA's own planner never shows an agency price.
- **The agency price is never in a cached answer** (review fix): the tour page is ISR-cached (`CATALOG_REVALIDATE_S`
  1800 s) and the browse Lambda answers `public, max-age=1800`, so a price in the browse body could stay up to about an
  hour after the agency switched prices off. The tour page now reads it client side on every view through the
  no-store `GET /api/trip/agency-price` -> assembly `GET /trip/agency-price?source_id=` (tenant from the BFF's tenant
  assertion, no guest token: no trip is read); the browse Lambda carries no agency price again.
- **Staleness window now** (review fix): `agency_prices.read` hides a list whose `received_at` is older than the
  setting `tripplanner.prices.agency_stale_hours` (CIS 476 seeds it: int 2..168, default 6; the CRM publishes every
  hour, so 6 h tolerates a few missed slots). A missing `received_at` is stale. The CRM also PUTs an empty hidden list
  when an agency stops being routed or Booking refuses it (AA-Partner side); the window covers a CRM that cannot
  publish at all (locked, deleted, down).
- **Code-health limits**: `handler.py` (1078) and `leads.py` (800) are at their ratchets, so the add-on routes live in
  `day_addons.serve` (called from `serve()` in place of `route()`, falling back to it), the BFF in its own route file
  (`/api/trip/addons`, added to the proxy's cookie paths), and docstrings were re-wrapped to keep the line counts.

## Changed

- Booking's view needed a name for the day cards: `bk-070` appends `title` to `booking.v_place_addons`
  (`supply.place_addons.v1` 1.1) — the `tripplanner` role reads no CIS supply content.
- The planner e-mail lists picks as "Add-on:" (the traveller-text brand guard refuses "AA" in backend text).

## Tradeoffs

- No date filter on the day cards: the planner has no trip date before the lead form, so a card lists the add-ons
  offered at the place within Booking's horizon, with their running days; the agency's quote checks the date.
- The planner's tour list cards show no agency price (the browse list is cached; a no-store read per card would be one
  call per card): a follow-up can add one batched no-store read for the visible cards.
- The tour page shows its price after a client read (no price in the server HTML, so no stale price in any cache).
