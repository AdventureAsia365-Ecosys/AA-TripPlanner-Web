# AA-886 (S245) — draft /privacy and /terms on the Trip Planner

Part A of the issue only (pages + links). The legal packet (part B) stays open.

## Decisions

- **Own pages, not redirects.** adventure.asia has a company privacy policy but no terms page, and its
  policy does not describe what the Trip Planner collects. Both pages are therefore served here (200),
  and the privacy notice links the company policy as the wider document.
- **Draft is enforced by the shell.** `components/site/LegalPage.tsx` always renders `DraftBanner`
  ("Draft — pending legal review"); a page cannot opt out except by not using the shell, which
  `lib/legal.test.ts` catches.
- **noindex + not in the sitemap** while the text is a draft (`robots: { index: false, follow: true }`).
  Search engines should not cache an unapproved legal text.
- **Links next to the PII form.** The contact form in `TripPanel` (name, phone, email, consent) is the
  only form that collects personal data. The links sit under the consent checkbox, outside the
  `<label>` so a click never toggles the checkbox, and open in a new tab so the typed form is kept.
  Link colour is `aa-ink` underlined (gold on white at 11 px fails WCAG AA contrast).
- **Open legal points stay visible** as `[to be confirmed]` (controller entity, legal basis, retention
  periods, transfer safeguards, minimum age, liability, governing law, booking conditions). These are
  counsel's decisions, not engineering's.

## Changed

- New: `frontend/lib/legal.ts` (paths, draft label, version/date, contact email, company policy URL),
  `frontend/components/site/LegalPage.tsx`, `frontend/app/privacy/page.tsx`, `frontend/app/terms/page.tsx`,
  `frontend/lib/legal.test.ts`.
- `frontend/components/site/SiteFooter.tsx`: Privacy and Terms links (footer nav now wraps on phones).
- `frontend/components/TripPanel.tsx`: "How we use your details: Privacy · Terms (draft)" under the
  consent checkbox.
- `CONTEXT.md`: pages section lists the legal pages.

## Tradeoffs

- Content is JSX in the pages rather than a Markdown/CMS source: two pages, no new dependency, and the
  kit styles apply directly. Moving to a shared source with the CIS portal was not worth it; the
  audiences and the data differ.
- Tests read the source files (node --test, no React renderer in this repo). They check the contract
  (shell, noindex, links), not the wording.
