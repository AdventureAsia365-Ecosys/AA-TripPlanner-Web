# AA-1094: an agency's planner shows the agency's own tours under the agency's brand

S278 system audit items M20 (TripPlanner read the AA catalogue for every tenant: `catalog.v_tenant_tour_v1` did not
exist) and M21 (the tenant planner linked to AA-branded `/tours/{slug}` pages with AA's price, and offered "Sign in"
through the frozen traveller client). Root ADR 0019 d10 / d11. CIS 403 creates the view (AA-CIS-App, migration PR of
this issue; contract root `docs/contracts/catalog.tenant_tour.v1.json`).

## Decisions

- **Which list a planner shows.** `/{slug}/plan` asks `resource=tenant-tours&tenant=<slug>`; the browse Lambda resolves
  the slug (served only) and answers the agency's rows of `catalog.v_tenant_tour_v1` (`source: "tenant"`). The demo
  tenant (kind `demo`, the AA-hosted `aa-tripplanner`) has no agency text, so it answers the AA catalogue
  (`source: "aa"`) and its links stay on AA's tour pages. The bare `/plan` is unchanged.
- **Where a tour opens.** `lib/tenantTours.ts` `tourHref`: the agency's own page `/{slug}/tours/{tour}` for a
  `tenant` list, AA's `/tours/{tour}` otherwise (also for an older BFF answer without `source`).
- **The agency's tour page.** Text from the view, AA photos and facts; no AA from-price (the agency is the merchant of
  record, b5); "Plan this trip" back to the agency's planner with the tour pre-filled; noindex for now (per-tenant
  canonical URLs are a later slice); the AI notice and a machine-readable mark (`data-ai-source="tenant"`, human-edited
  flag) on the AI-written blocks.
- **Brand.** The agency's bar replaces the AA bar on its pages (trading name; the view has no logo field yet, so no
  logo), with an AI pill whose explanation does not name Adventure Asia. AA pages keep the AA bar.
- **No Sign in anywhere.** d11 makes travellers guests and freezes the EXTERNAL client, so `AccountLink` renders only
  "My trips" for a traveller who still has a session, and nothing on an agency's pages. `/sign-in` and `/trips` stay
  reachable by URL (removing them with the account BFF is the retirement of the frozen client, a separate change).
- **The tab's tenant is the page's.** `PlannerApp` sets the tenant in an effect too and clears it on unmount, so
  leaving the planner client-side cannot carry the agency onto AA pages.
- **View absent = empty list, not an error** (before CIS 403 is applied), like the 379 lookup.

## Changed

- Backend: `backend/browse/tenant_tours.py` (new), two routes in `backend/browse/handler.py`.
- Frontend: `lib/tenantTours.ts` (new), `app/[tenant]/tours/[tour]/page.tsx` (new), `app/api/browse/route.ts`
  (`tenant-tours`), `lib/api.ts`, `components/TourListPane.tsx`, `components/Header.tsx` (agency bar),
  `components/AccountLink.tsx`, `components/AiLabel.tsx`, `app/plan/PlannerApp.tsx`, types and the server getter.
- `e2e/visual/pages.ts`: the new route is excluded from the automatic visual run with its reason (no agency with a
  finished T2 version can be resolved from a page link); the live check captures it.

## Tradeoffs

- The map, the search and the route finder still read the AA graph views on an agency's planner: the agency sees AA's
  places and routes there; only the tour list and tour pages are the agency's. Scoping the graph per tenant needs
  tenant variants of the graph views (a later minor).
- The tenant page is cached like the catalog pages (tagged, 30 min): an agency's new text shows within 30 minutes; a
  retire / pause from CIS drops it at once (the lifecycle purge tag).
