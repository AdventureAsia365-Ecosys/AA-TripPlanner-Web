# AA-674 — Multi-tour composition + route-constrained "suggest next" (PR-11)

## Step 1 — rebuild itinerary_components for the active tours

### Decisions
- **From atoms, deterministic** (`backend/extraction/components.py`, op `components`): each atom
  already carries `place` + `activity_type`, so `categorize_atom` maps it to one component with no
  model call. Lodging and transit "places" are skipped (the same rule as the night anchor).
- **Places are linked through `locate.py`**: rows located before (`located_by`, migration 005)
  are reused by name; legacy rows and new names are located (model + Mapbox, one call per 40
  names). Components whose place cannot be located are dropped.
- **Embeddings** via `tp_component_embed`, 96 texts per call, logged. Search and taste ranking
  read `embedding IS NOT NULL` rows only.
- **Replace per tour in one transaction**, one tour per Lambda invoke. Only active tours are
  touched; the 1,081 legacy components of unpublished tours stay until an opt-in prune.
- Reading atoms needs CIS migration 180 (column grant to `tripplanner`).

## Step 2 — route-constrained "suggest next"

### Decisions
- **Candidates follow real tours** (`ROUTE_CANDIDATES_SQL` in `backend/assembly/suggestions.py`):
  - take every `tour_stop` within `JUNCTION_KM` (150) of the trip's last stop (the last stop is
    sequenced as before);
  - for each such tour, the candidates are the components of the days after that stop, up to the
    tour's next stop at a different destination (a multi-night stay is skipped over).
  So a suggestion is always "what an Adventure Asia tour does next from here", and a stop
  > 1 km away is a transfer to another tour (the chaining PR-11 asks for).
- **Ranking unchanged**: taste (pgvector) blended with distance to the last stop. The candidate
  pool is just narrower.
- **Fallback** to the old taste-in-trip-countries query when no tour passes near the last stop.
  The response says which one ran (`mode`).
- The "next stop" is the tour's next stop *elsewhere than that stop*, not outside the whole
  150 km circle. The first version used the circle and returned nothing in Bhutan, where Paro,
  Thimphu and Punakha all sit within 150 km of each other.

### Changed
- Fixed: `events._current_components` did not select `destination_id`, so pinned destinations
  were never excluded from suggestions.

### Should know
- Measured on Dev as the `tripplanner` role: ~25 ms. From Kathmandu: Lukla, Pokhara, Namche
  Bazaar, Phakding, Everest Base Camp. From Thimphu: Kurje Lhakhang, Kyichu Lhakhang, Buddha
  Dordenma.
- Filters (country, activity, intensity, duration) and whole-leg-chain suggestions are step 3.

## Step 3a — coverage check + whole-route proposals

### Decisions
- **Coverage** (`backend/assembly/coverage.py`, `GET /trip/{id}/coverage`):
  - pins are taken in trip order; each pin may be served by any (tour, day) whose components visit
    its destination;
  - a small dynamic programme picks one per pin, minimising gaps (a hop > 150 km between tours),
    then transfers, then days;
  - returns legs (tour, days, tour name), transfers (km, ok) and gaps with a reason.
  - Deterministic, no model call.
- **Route proposals** (`backend/browse/routes.py`, `GET /browse/routes?country&days`), each either:
  - one leg of one tour, or
  - two legs of two tours chained at a junction (the first leg starting at its tour's first stop).
  Total days fall in [days − 3, days], and a whole tour (first to last stop) ranks first.
  Ranked by closeness to the duration, then fewer transfers, then more places. Each proposal
  carries a day-by-day list with coordinates for the map polyline. Browse Lambda, cacheable.

### Tradeoffs
- Chains are limited to two tours. Three-tour chains grow the search a lot, and the planner can
  still extend a proposal with suggest-next.
- Measured on Dev as the `tripplanner` role: 30–41 ms per request (single + chain SQL).
  - Bhutan 7 d → the whole "Valley Hikes & Mountain Passes" tour first.
  - Sri Lanka 12 d → three whole 10–11-day tours.
  - Nepal 10 d: only cuts of 14–18-day tours exist in the current 56-tour subset.
- Params are cast `::int` (asyncpg sent `$2 - $3` as unknown − unknown).

## Step 3b — suggestion filters, activity selection, added days (PR-11 points 2 and 3)

### Decisions
- **Activity selection needs no new event.** A component *is* a place + activity of a real tour
  day, so selecting is `add_component` and deselecting is `remove_component`.
  - New `GET /trip/{id}/stops/{destination_id}/activities` lists what AA tours do at that stop,
    one entry per (name, activity), with `tour_count` and `selected`.
  - Anything not listed (no tour offers it there) cannot be selected.
- **Added days** are events `add_day {day_id, kind, after_component_id, note}` / `remove_day`.
  - Kinds: `extra_night` (must follow a pinned stop), `free_day`, `extend_end`.
  - `POST /trip/{id}/days` and `DELETE /trip/{id}/days/{day_id}`.
  - They are customizations (not part of any tour): `GET /trip/{id}` returns `custom_days`, and
    coverage adds them to `total_days` (`leg_days` stays the tours' days).
  - The advisor email already lists the whole event log, so selections and added days reach the
    advisor.
- Migration 006 widens `trip_events_type_chk` (applied on Dev 29/09).
- **Suggestion filters** `?activity=&intensity_level=` apply to both the route and the taste
  query. Unknown values are ignored. GET query strings now reach routes through `body`.

### Should know
- Measured on Dev as `tripplanner`. Kathmandu offers 4 selectable activities. Suggestions:
  - no filter: Lukla, Pokhara, Namche (44 ms);
  - `trekking`: Everest Base Camp, Lukla, Sagarmatha (16 ms);
  - `culinary`: none on the next legs (4 ms).
