# AA-811 — Advisor e-mail moves to AA-Booking (TripPlanner half)

## Decisions

- **AA-Booking sends the staff "new inquiry" e-mail, TripPlanner sends none** (Nghiep, S238; ADR 0004 boundary:
  TripPlanner is the front door, Booking owns customers, trip cases and notifications). Booking queues job
  `bk_notify_email` when it processes the `inquiry.submitted` outbox row that "Send to an advisor" writes.
- `backend/assembly/notify.py` keeps the `Sender` seam and `LoggingSender`, which now also writes one INFO log line
  per hand-off ("logged, not e-mailed"), so CloudWatch shows the hand-off. No SES sender is added here.
- `ADVISOR_NOTIFY_EMAIL` defaults to empty (it was a personal address); it only labels that log line.

## Changed vs. the issue

- Issue scope 4 ("wire TripPlanner `notify.py` advisor email as the first real consumer") is superseded: the first
  real consumer is AA-Booking. AA-CIS-Infra removes the `ADVISOR_NOTIFY_EMAIL` Lambda env and its variable (same
  issue, separate PR). The Lambda role keeps its SES send grant until someone decides TripPlanner never mails.

## Tradeoffs

- Keeping `notify_advisor` and the handler call (instead of deleting them) keeps the diff small and the hand-off log
  line; deleting the seam is a later clean-up.

## Should know

- Live check of the whole chain is on the Booking side: hand-off → `booking.job` `bk_notify_email` succeeded with an
  SES `message_id` → e-mail in the staff inbox.
