# AA-820 — Trip Planner terms: who writes the AI text and the Anthropic Usage Policy (S266)

Source: root `docs/legal/tenant-ai-schedule.md` v1.0 §2 (c) (approved by the tech lead under root ADR 0019 b20) and the
root AI Use Register row `tp_compose` (Claude through Amazon Bedrock, no fallback outside AWS).

## Decisions

- **Travellers are end users, not tenants.** The AI Schedule binds agencies; the Trip Planner terms only gain one
  bullet in §3 "Use of AI": the narration is written by Anthropic's Claude through Amazon Bedrock on Adventure Asia's
  own account, and the Anthropic Usage Policy (effective 12/11/2026, which covers "the end users of products or
  services integrating Claude") applies to the site's AI features too.
- **Version bump.** `LEGAL_VERSION` Draft v0.1 → v0.2, `LEGAL_UPDATED` 3 November 2026 (also fixes the known
  follow-up that the pages changed after 20/10/2026 but still showed that date). The draft banner stays.

## Changed

- `frontend/app/terms/page.tsx` §3, `frontend/lib/legal.ts` (`ANTHROPIC_USAGE_POLICY_URL`, version), test in
  `frontend/lib/legal.test.ts`.

## Tradeoffs

- The B2C wording elsewhere on the pages (an Adventure Asia advisor confirms the trip) is left for the R5 tenant-sited
  Trip Planner work (ADR 0019 d10/d11); this slice changes only the AI section.
