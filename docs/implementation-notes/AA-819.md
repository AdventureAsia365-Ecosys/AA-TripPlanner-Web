# AA-819 item 4 — TripPlanner AI labels (S239)

Scope: AUP 2026-11 scope item 4 ("every AI-generated text block the traveller sees carries an honest
AI label, on touch and keyboard"). Input: the S239 audit
`AA-Ecosys/docs/audits/2026-10-16-S239-in-progress-audit/ui-verify/aa819-tp-badge/README.md`,
defects D1–D6. Items 1–3 of AA-819 (portal notice, T9 guard, publish limit) are CIS work, not here.

> The sections below describe the first lane commit; where "Review round 1" at the end differs
> (day resolution, label wording, narration badge), round 1 wins.

## Decisions

- **One label component, `components/AiLabel.tsx`.** A `<button aria-expanded aria-controls>` that
  toggles a short explanation (`role="note"`); Escape or a tap/click outside closes it. Two tones
  (`planner` = the light-only `aa-*` palette, `kit` = theme-aware `k-*` tokens), two variants
  (solid / outline, the two looks the old badges had), two placements: `popover` (floats under the
  label; headers, section headings) and `inline` (opens in the flow; used inside the map popup,
  which clips overflow, and in the stop activity list). Min height 24 px (WCAG 2.5.8 target size);
  text 10 px instead of 9 px. No `title` attribute any more: the explanation is the one source.
- **Wording (D2, D5).** Only what is really AI is named.
  - Site pill: tour descriptions and itineraries are drafted with AI and reviewed; "AI narration" /
    "AA + AI" narration is written by AI on request; the planner's place search uses AI (Cohere
    embedding) to match words. Suggestions are **not** mentioned: they are rule-based
    (`backend/assembly/suggestions.py`).
  - Narration `master`: label kept as "AA itinerary", explanation "Adventure Asia's own itinerary
    text, written with AI assistance and reviewed by our team." (the text is the CIS S1 writer's
    reviewed output, `published_tours.aa_itineraries`). `mixed` "AA + AI" and `llm` "AI narration"
    keep their meaning; `basic` (budget spent, neutral name lines) still has no label.
  - Catalog text (tour page, popup, stop activities): "AI-assisted, reviewed by Adventure Asia",
    explanation "Drafted by an AI writing model from Adventure Asia's own tour notes, then reviewed by
    our team. Your advisor confirms every detail before you book."
- **Tour page (D4): three labels** — under the summary, next to "Highlights", next to "Day by day"
  (only when there is day text or legacy itinerary text). One label per block, as asked; a single
  page-level label would not sit next to the text it qualifies.
- **D3: title apart from prose.** `parseNarration` moved to `lib/narration.ts` (unit-tested) and now
  returns `{day, title, text}`. A first line is a title when it is ≤ 90 chars and does not end with
  `.!?…`; AI-written days are one line and get no title. The split also only starts a block at a
  `Day N` marker at the start of a line (a "Day 2 - …" inside prose no longer splits a block).
- **D1 root cause and fix: see "Should know".** Fix in `master_content.resolve_day_text`: the authored
  day text is used only when it names the stop (component name or `shared.destinations` name,
  case/accent/punctuation-insensitive, whole words). Else the nearest day of the same tour that does
  (ties: the earlier day). Else the day goes to the LLM (or the neutral line when the budget is spent).
  An entry with no name at all cannot be checked and keeps its own day (old behaviour).
- **Days are resolved per trip day**, from the first of the day's entries whose text resolves; only
  the entries of an unresolved day go to the LLM (before, a day could be narrated from master text
  and still send its other entries to the model).
- **The narration cache key uses the resolved text** (`narration_cache._master_text` →
  `resolve_day_text`), so an edit to the day actually used misses the cache. `PROMPT_VERSION` bumped
  to `2026-10-16.1` so cached `mixed` narrations built with a wrong-day text or a truncated merge are
  not served again.

## Tradeoffs

- **Name matching can send a correct day to the LLM** when neither the component name nor the place
  name appears in that day's text (paraphrase). That costs a model call (inside the daily cap) instead
  of showing another place's text under the stop. Measured on the live catalog (public BFF, read
  only): of the 17 pinned-component days on the India tours that have a Tour Graph route, 16 name
  their stop on their own day and 1 (Amber Fort, Rajasthan 22 days, day 22 = "Departure from Delhi")
  does not; that one is repaired to day 16 by the nearest-day rule, so 0 of 17 would go to the LLM.
  Small sample; watch `shared.llm_call_log` (`tp_compose`) after deploy.
- **Repair instead of reject.** Using another day of the same tour keeps AA text (no cost) and is
  what the traveller expects for that place; the risk is a tour that visits the place twice, where the
  nearest day wins.
- **Labels on the tour page are three, not one.** Slightly busier, but each block is labelled where
  it is read, which is what the AUP item asks.
- **Tour cards (home, destination pages, planner list) are not labelled.** Their 2-line teaser is
  inside a link, where a button is invalid HTML; the site pill covers them and the full text on the
  tour page is labelled.

## Review round 1 (S239 lane review, 7 findings, all real)

### Decisions

- **Day resolution is per itinerary, not per entry** (`master_content.resolve_days`, replacing
  `resolve_day_text`). Per trip day: (1) the own tour day text of *any* of the day's entries that names
  that entry's stop; (2) else the nearest other day of the same tour that names the stop and that is
  **free**: no entry of the itinerary has it as its own day and no earlier trip day took it; (3) else
  the own day text as it is; (4) only a day with no AA text at all goes to the LLM. Why: in a
  "Customise this trip" (tour_graph) itinerary every tour day is somebody's own day, so a loose pin
  ("Cho La Pass" on the EBC departure day, "Phuket" on the elephant-sanctuary day) used to copy a
  neighbour's text in twice and drop the real day. And a day that names no stop is more often a
  spelling variant ("Koh Phi Phi" for "Koh Phi Phi Don", "Pashupatinath" for "Pashupatinath Temple")
  than a wrong day, so it keeps AA text instead of costing a model call. The D1 Amber Fort case still
  redirects (day 16 is free in that trip).
- **Looser name match:** a second key drops trailing generic words (`temple`, `fort`, `street`,
  `don`, `pass`, `lake`, …) when what remains has >= 4 letters.
- **Wording states only what is true.** CIS publishes S1 output that passes the automated gate
  (`_is_publishable` → `process_export`, AA-CIS-App `api/routers/admin_pipeline.py`); only failing rows
  reach the A2 Review Queue. So no label claims a team review: catalog label is now "AI-assisted"
  ("Written by an AI model from Adventure Asia's own tour notes and checked by automated quality
  checks; tours the checks flag are reviewed by our team. Your advisor confirms every detail before
  you book."). Constants renamed `AI_CATALOG_LABEL` / `AI_CATALOG_EXPLANATION`. The advisor hand-off
  review ("An Adventure Asia advisor reviews every trip") is true and stays.
- **The visible narration badge carries the AI fact:** `master` = "AA itinerary · AI-assisted".
  The API's `ai_generated` is now true for `master` too (`_AI_SOURCES`); nothing in the frontend read
  it, so this is additive for consumers.
- **New narration source `master_basic`:** budget spent but some days are AA text. It gets the
  "AA itinerary · AI-assisted" badge with an explanation of the limit. `basic` stays for "neutral
  lines only" (no badge, `ai_generated` false). Neither is cached (unchanged).
- **Narration badge moved to its own row and opens inline** (`placement="inline"`): the trip panel
  scrolls, and a floating 256 px panel from mid-row was clipped and scrolled the panel sideways.
- **Site pill names suggestions as AI too:** suggestions rank by the pinned components' Cohere
  embedding taste vector (route-constrained), the same technology as place search.

### Tradeoffs

- Keeping a non-matching own day (rule 3) means a genuinely wrong day with no free alternative still
  shows the wrong text (e.g. Amber Fort if the tour's day 16 were missing or pinned by another stop).
  Accepted: the reviewer's live sample showed far more name variants than wrong days, and the
  alternative was a paid AI line or a duplicated day.
- Live read-only check of the new rule on the public catalog (`aa-tripplanner.vercel.app` tour-route +
  tour page day texts, 10 tour_graph tours in Nepal/Thailand/India/Vietnam, 95 pinned days, whole-tour
  itineraries): 0 days sent to the LLM, 0 duplicated day texts, 0 redirects. Caveat as in the review:
  the component name is approximated by the route day name.
