# AA-1130 — Agency planner page fixes (S283 UI audit, batch T1)

Source: root `docs/audits/2026-11-17-S283-fe-audit/README.md` (batch T1) and `rows.json` (app `tripplanner`).

## Decisions

- **Filter, not hide, by default.** The agency planner's route finder stays and proposes only the tours the agency
  sells (its rows of `catalog.v_tenant_tour_v1`). It is hidden only when the agency sells no tour. The audit asked
  for the tech lead's call on whether an agency may propose AA tours it has not adopted. The lane brief chose
  "default = filter". No setting allows unadopted AA tours. A later per-tenant switch would be a CIS tenant setting,
  not an env var.
- **The scope rides on the query (`tenant=<slug>`), unsigned.** The scope is the agency's public tour list, which
  `GET /browse/tenant/{slug}/tours` already serves without a signature. The Lambda resolves the slug through the
  served-tenant lookup first. An unknown slug is 404, a read that cannot answer is 503, and the unscoped AA list is
  never the answer.
- **Scoped SQL variants, not a nullable fourth parameter.** `SINGLE_SCOPED_SQL` / `CHAIN_SCOPED_SQL` add
  `AND l.source_id = ANY($4::text[])` to the leg reads. The unscoped reads keep their exact text and three
  parameters, so AA's plans and the AA-847 equivalence test do not move. In the chain read, both legs come from the
  filtered `legs` CTE, so a chain never borrows a tour the agency lacks.
- **Agency titles replace AA titles.** On an agency planner a proposal segment's `tour_name` is the agency's
  `title` from the view. On tour-route (the "Plan this trip" pre-fill) the title is the agency's too, and the "not on
  the map" fallback drops the day titles, which are AA text. A tour-route for a tour the agency does not sell answers
  404 with `tour: null`.
- **`tour_count` on `/browse/countries`.** It counts live catalogue tours per country (`catalog.v_tour`, the same
  liveness as the home page's country list), matched on the lower-cased name. On an agency planner the list is cut
  to the countries of its tours, with the agency's own count. "Heading to" lists only countries with `tour_count > 0`.
  The map's country filter (FilterChips) keeps every country with experiences on AA's planner.
- **Legal links in the agency bar.** The planner is full-height and has no footer, so the agency bar
  (`AgencyHeader`) carries Privacy and Terms on every `/{slug}` page. The AA-hosted demo tenant links AA's pages, as
  its forms do. The tenant tour page also ends with a legal line (Privacy, Terms, Use of AI), and its AI notice
  links the agency's "Use of AI" terms.
- **"Full route · days 1–7 of 8".** `routes.proposals()` sends `tour_days` (the tour's `duration_days`) for a
  one-tour proposal, and `null` for a chain. The badge says "Whole tour" only when the route covers every day of the
  tour.
- **Map/List toggle.** It is raised (`bottom-16`) wherever the map is narrow: phones, and the side-by-side layout up
  to `xl`, where the map is the viewport minus the 25rem panel and still meets the attribution at 1024. From `xl` it
  stays at `bottom-4`. The tour pre-fill box moves up with it (`bottom-28`, `xl:bottom-16`).

## Tradeoffs

- **Unsigned `tenant`.** Anyone can ask for an agency's scoped route list, but that list is the same public data the
  agency's own tour list shows. Signing these reads would mean a signed header on cacheable reads for no gain.
- **Cache keys.** The scope is part of the URL, so each agency's answer caches on its own key. A tour an agency adopts
  shows in its finder within the browse TTL (the same as its tour list).
- **Name match on country.** Tour country (from the source Excel row) and destination country are matched on the
  lower-cased name, as `catalog.COUNTRY_COVERS_SQL` already does. Today all 14 tour countries match a destination
  country.

## Should know

- **The 7 missing country cover photos** (India, Japan, Laos, Philippines, Thailand, Pakistan, Vietnam) are not a
  TripPlanner mapping problem. Live check on 19/11 (`/api/browse?resource=catalog-tours&country=<c>` and
  `resource=destination`):
  - no tour in those countries has its own cover (`cover_source` is never `tour`);
  - the destinations carry the same country name (for example, Delhi and Red Fort are "India") but have no
    `cover_photo_path` or `cover_image_url`;
  - so `catalog.COUNTRY_COVERS_SQL` finds nothing to fall back to;
  - the 2 India tours that have a cover show Kandy (Sri Lanka) and Kathmandu (Nepal).

  The fix is photo coverage on the CIS side: tour photo matching and destination photos for those countries.
  It is recorded as a CIS follow-up.
- **Still AA text on an agency planner.** The trip panel's coverage legs (`leg.tour_name` from the assembly Lambda),
  the map, search and stop activities still read AA's graph, as recorded in ADR 0019 S278. The coverage leg names
  are the remaining AA tour names an agency traveller can see after pinning stops. This is recorded as a follow-up
  (assembly Lambda, same `served_scope`).
- The docs-audit check on `/sign-in` and `/trips` found no live link or page. Only comments, the local env example
  and the README env row still described them, and they are updated here. `lib/tenantSlug.ts` keeps both slugs
  reserved on purpose.
